HOW TO NOT PLAY

Avoidle is Wordle, except your one job is to not guess the word. Landing on it by accident isn't a win, it's the whole game ending in shame.

You can't just type nonsense to dodge it, though. Every guess still has to respect what you've already learned:

Right letter, right spot. It's locked in now, every future guess has to keep it exactly there.
Right letter, wrong spot. It has to show up somewhere else in your next guesses.
Not in the word. Stop wasting rows on it.

The possible answers left counter is judging you the whole time. It only shrinks. When it gets close to 1, you're basically one careless keystroke from accidentally winning.

Same word for everyone, new one every day at midnight. Survive all 6 rows without saying it.
