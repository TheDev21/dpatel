REVERSE 21

  • You start at 21. Every card you draw subtracts from your total instead of adding.
  • Number cards subtract their face value. Jack, Queen, King each subtract 10.
  • Ace subtracts 1 or 11, whichever keeps you from going below zero.
  • Go below 0 and you bust — instant loss.
  • Hit to draw another card, Stand to lock in your total and let the house play.
  • The house keeps drawing until its own total would stand at 17 or better in regular blackjack terms — here, until it's down to 4 or less.
  • Whoever lands closer to 0 without busting wins. Same total is a push.
